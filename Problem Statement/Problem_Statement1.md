# Retail & B2B Wholesale Distribution – Business Analysis

## Final 10 Business Questions

1. **Campaign Performance:** Which marketing campaigns perform best and worst in terms of spending, clicks, and associated sales trends over time?

2. **Sales Channel Performance:** Which sales channels generate the highest revenue and gross profit across different customer regions?

3. **Customer Profitability:** Which customers contribute the most and least to overall gross profit?

4. **Discount Impact:** How do different discount levels affect revenue and gross profit across product categories and customer regions?

5. **Marketing Spending Efficiency:** Which marketing channels generate the highest click-through rates relative to their advertising spend?

6. **Promoted Product Performance:** How do promoted products perform in terms of sales revenue and gross profit across product categories and customer regions?

7. **Inventory vs Sales Demand:** Which products have high sales demand relative to their available inventory levels?

8. **Inventory Distribution:** How is inventory distributed across products and categories, and which products have the highest and lowest stock levels?

9. **Shipping Efficiency:** Which months and customer regions experience the longest order processing, shipping, and delivery times?

10. **Revenue and Profitability Trends:** How do monthly revenue, gross profit, and gross profit margins change over time, and how does actual revenue compare with sales targets?

## Data Limitations

- **Q1 & Q6:** Campaign-attributed revenue is unavailable. Analyse promotional coverage and sales patterns without claiming direct campaign attribution.
- **Q7:** Inventory snapshots can be compared with sales demand, but cannot conclusively identify lost sales due to stockouts.
- **Q9:** Analyse order processing, shipping, and delivery durations. Customer payment-cycle analysis is outside the current scope.

## Overall Business Objective

Improve revenue target achievement and gross profitability by evaluating marketing effectiveness, customer and product profitability, inventory management, sales channel performance, and order fulfilment efficiency.
